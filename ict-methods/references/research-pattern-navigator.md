# Research pattern navigator

Many research projects can be described in terms of one or more research patterns: each pattern is a combination of research strategies. Match the question you're trying to answer to a pattern below.

| Question | Strategies involved | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| How do I get a grasp on what the stakeholders really want? | field, workshop | [Clarify focus and scope](patterns/clarify-focus-and-scope.md) |
| How can I help the stakeholders get clear what they really want? | field, workshop | [Clarify focus and scope](patterns/clarify-focus-and-scope.md) |
| How do I develop a deep enough understanding of the context using available work? | field, library | [Solidify](patterns/solidify.md) |
| How do I monitor the quality of my solution? | workshop, lab | [Validate](patterns/validate.md) |
| How do I choose the right technologies for my project? | library, field, workshop | [Choose fitting technology](patterns/choose-fitting-technology.md) |
| How can I ensure the product meets the needs of the stakeholders throughout the project? | field, workshop, lab | [Realise as required](patterns/realise-as-required.md) |
| How do I develop a solution that is more generally applicable than just for my immediate stakeholders? | library, workshop | [Realise as an expert](patterns/realise-as-an-expert.md) |
| How do I develop a state-of-the-art solution that meets professional requirements? | library, workshop | [Realise as an expert](patterns/realise-as-an-expert.md) |
| How do I develop something using technologies or techniques that I don't know yet? | library, workshop | [Realise as an expert](patterns/realise-as-an-expert.md) |
